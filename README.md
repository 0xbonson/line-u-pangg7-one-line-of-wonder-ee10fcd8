# One Line of Wonder

```
The chalk in a child's hand is the crushed shells of ocean creatures who spent their lives drifting toward sunlight.
```

A line evoking deep wonder by connecting an everyday tool of childhood learning to ancient marine life floating toward the light of the sun millions of years ago.
